Nivedha Infotec - Employee Benefits Policy

This handbook defines the standard employee benefits offered by Nivedha
Infotec for full-time, trainer, SME, and project staff.

1. Health & Insurance Benefits

-   Comprehensive medical insurance coverage for employees and
    dependents
-   Accidental insurance and term life insurance included
-   Annual preventive health check-up reimbursement

2. Financial Benefits

-   Provident Fund (PF) and Employee State Insurance (ESI) as per
    statutory norms
-   Annual performance bonus based on appraisal ratings
-   Trainer deployment incentives and SME evaluation bonuses

3. Learning & Development

-   Certification reimbursement for approved technologies
-   Access to premium learning platforms and internal LMS
-   Quarterly trainer upskilling workshops and client-specific bootcamps

4. Work-Life Benefits

-   Hybrid work option based on project/client needs
-   Internet and home-office allowance for remote employees
-   Wellness sessions, counseling, and employee assistance programs

5. Travel & Client Support Benefits

-   Travel reimbursement for client visits and training delivery
-   Daily allowance for onsite deployments
-   Hotel and transport coverage for outstation assignments
